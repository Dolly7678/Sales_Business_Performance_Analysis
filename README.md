#Sales_Business_Performance_Analysis
#Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing sales and business performance using data analytics tools such as Excel, PostgreSQL and Power BI.

The objective of this project is to analyze sales trends, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and regional business performance to generate meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

The project uses three datasets: Customers, Products, and Orders.

#Business Objectives
. Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
. Identify top-performing products and categories.
. Understand customer purchasing behavior.

Compare sales and profitability across different states.
. Analyze sales trends over time.
. Evaluate customer segments based on sales contribution.

Identify business opportunities and areas for improvement.

#Tools & Technologies
. Microsoft Excel
. Data validation, cleaning, and initial analysis
. PostgreSQL
. Data storage, joins, and SQL analysis
. Business queries, aggregations, and performance analysis
Power BI
. Data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive dashboard

Dataset Description
The project contains three datasets:

1. Customers
Customer details such as Customer ID, Customer Name, City, State, Age, Gender, and Segment.

2. Products
Product details such as Product ID, Product Name, Category, Sub-Category, and Price.

3. Orders
Transaction details such as Order ID, Order Date, Customer ID, Product ID, Quantity, Discount, Sales, and Profit.

 Project Workflow
Phase 1: Data Cleaning – Excel
Checked missing values and duplicate records.
Validated data types and column formats.
Checked customer and product identifiers.
Prepared datasets for analysis.

Phase 2: SQL Analysis – PostgreSQL
. Created database tables.
. Performed JOIN operations across Customers, Products, and Orders.
. Used aggregate functions such as SUM, AVG, COUNT, and GROUP BY.
. Analyzed sales by category, state, and customer segment.
. Identified top-performing products and customers.
. Analyzed monthly sales trends.

Phase 3: Power BI Dashboard
. Created relationships between Customers, Products, and Orders.
. Created a Calendar table for time-based analysis.
. Developed DAX measures for key performance indicators.
Designed an interactive dashboard for business performance analysis.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
. Total Sales
. Total Profit
. Total Orders
. Total Customers
. Total Quantity Sold
. Profit Margin %
. Average Order Value (AOV)

Business Insights
. The analysis is designed to help answer the following business questions:
. Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
. Which states contribute the most to total sales?
. Which customer segment generates the highest profit?
. What are the monthly sales and profit trends?
. Which products have the highest sales?
. How does discounting affect profitability?
 
Conclusion

This project demonstrates the practical application of data analytics tools to solve business problems.

By combining Excel, PostgreSQL and Power BI, the project transforms raw business data into meaningful insights that can support better decision-making, improve profitability, and identify growth opportunities.
